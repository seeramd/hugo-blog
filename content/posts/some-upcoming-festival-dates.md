I'll traveling to perform in some festivals this Fall

### Saturday, October 3rd, 6:30 PM
#### HaHa Harvest Comedy Festival @ Jersey City, NJ

This is an annual festival that takes place in Jersey City and Hoboken. I was fortunate enough to be selected for this fest last year, and I'm quite pleased to be back in 2026.

You can grab tickets for the show I'm on [here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/comedy-cornucopia-tickets-2001000981802). For a full listing of all the fest's shows, you can go [here](https://www.eventbrite.com/o/ha-ha-harvest-comedy-17984969650).

### Friday, October 16th, 6:30 PM
#### Crooked Mouth Comedy Festival @ Endicott, NY

An annual festival that takes place in the Crooked Mouth Brewery. This is another fest where I performed last year, and it was a great time. This year the show I'm on will be headlined by **Chris Gethard**, who I'm excited to see live.

You can grab a ticket to the show I'm on [here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/cmcf26-night-2-early-show-with-chris-gethard-tickets-1990184127299?aff=erelpanelorg). You can find info, passes, and tickets to the other events on the [festival website](https://www.crookedmouthcomedyfest.com/).

### Friday, November 13th, 6:30 PM
### Saturday, November 14th, 5:00 PM and 7:30 PM
#### Helena Comedy Festival @ Helena, MT

I'm fortunate to be featured in the second annual Helena Comedy Festival in Helena, Montana. I heard great things about the first festival, so I'm very glad to be accepted again this year. 

You can grab tickets/passes to the shows I'm on and and all other festivals events [here](https://helenacomedyfestival.com/#schedule).
